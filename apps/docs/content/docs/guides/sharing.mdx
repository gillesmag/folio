title: Share documents and collect feedback
description: Choose who can read a document and use comments to review its content.
---

## Share with an organization

1. Open **Organizations** from your profile menu.
2. Create an organization with a name and unique slug. You can create one organization and join others.
3. Open the organization and invite people by email.
4. Ask each person to sign in with the invited email and accept under **Organizations**. Invitations expire after seven days. Folio does not send email.
5. Open a document, select **Share**, choose the organization, and save.

Organization members can read and comment on its documents. You keep ownership and control of edits, sharing, and deletion. To remove organization access, move the document back to **Personal** in the Share dialog. Use **Private** link access to restrict reading to you and the selected organization's members.

The document list labels each document's organization. Use its dropdown to filter by Personal or an organization.

To upload from the CLI, pass the organization's slug or ID:

```sh
folio push notes.md --org acme
folio push notes.md --id DOCUMENT_ID --org org_abc123
```

Find the slug and ID in the organization's settings. Without `--org`, every CLI upload goes to Personal, including replacements with `--id`.

The organization creator can remove members. Members can leave from the organization's settings. Their documents return to Personal when they leave or are removed.

## Share a document by link

To let anyone with the link read your document, publish it as unlisted:

```sh
folio push notes.md --id DOCUMENT_ID --visibility unlisted
```

Share the URL printed by the CLI. Recipients can read it without signing in. Treat an unlisted URL as shareable access to the document.

To restrict access to your account, change the visibility to private in the web editor. You can also republish your local source:

```sh
folio push notes.md --id DOCUMENT_ID --visibility private
```

Document pages check current permissions on each request.

Public and unlisted documents have the same read access in the current implementation. Folio does not provide a public discovery feed. See the [API access rules](/docs/api/authentication) for details.

## Comment on a block

1. Sign in to the instance that hosts the document.
2. Open a document you can read.
3. Switch on **Comments** in the header. Comments are hidden by default; the toggle shows the number of open comments.
4. Select a block in the document.
5. Enter your comment and submit it.

To comment on the whole document, clear the selected block before submitting. The comment author and document owner can resolve or delete a comment.

## Read feedback from the terminal

```sh
folio comments DOCUMENT_ID
```

To process comments in a script, request JSON:

```sh
folio comments DOCUMENT_ID --json
```

The CLI lists comments. To create, resolve, or delete comments from a script, use the [API](/docs/api).
